Real scream recordings used for the jump scares (both CC0 / public domain, from OpenGameArt; credit given anyway):

- `scream_1..4.wav` - cut from "Female high-pitched scream SFX" by WuxiaScrub - https://opengameart.org/content/female-high-pitched-scream-sfx (CC0)
