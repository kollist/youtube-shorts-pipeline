# Sound credits

Real scream recordings used for the jump scares (both CC0 / public domain, from OpenGameArt; credit given anyway):

- `scream_1..4.wav` - cut from "Female high-pitched scream SFX" by WuxiaScrub - https://opengameart.org/content/female-high-pitched-scream-sfx (CC0)
- `scream_5..6.wav` - cut from "Horror scream1" by Vinrax - https://opengameart.org/content/horror-scream1 (CC0)
- `knock_1..3.wav` - wood hits 05, 07 and 09 from "100 CC0 metal and wood SFX" by rubberduck - https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx (CC0)

Everything else (foley, beds, stings) is synthesized in code (`horror_sfx.py`).
