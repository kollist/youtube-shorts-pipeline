Instagram and Facebook search the **display name**, not the @username. So put the keyword in the name:

- **Foundation Tapes | SCP Stories**   <- recommended

- The Foundation Tapes

## Username (check availability in the app - I cannot check it from here)

In order of preference: `foundationtapes`, `foundation.tapes`, `foundationtapes_`, `thefoundationtapes`, `tapes.foundation`, `scp.tapes`.
Avoid putting "official" or the SCP Foundation's own name or logo in the handle: the wiki's licence allows the stories, but not implying you are the wiki.
